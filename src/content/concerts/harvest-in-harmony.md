A fall tradition, the Orchestra will premiere original works and feature Jacoby Scarano, the 2025 Fredric and Susan Winthrop Young Artist.

The Orchestra on the Hill directed by Thomas Palance will be appearing at The Crane Estate on November 12th at 7 pm with a pre-concert talk at 6:30 pm. ***Harvest in Harmony*** will feature soloists from the Orchestra, as well as the 2025 recipient of **The Fredric and Susan Winthrop Young Artist Award, Jacoby Scarano**. Known for its tradition of new music, the Orchestra will premiere original works in the intimate setting of the estate's grand ballroom.

New compositions by: G.Paul Naeger, Jerry Gates, Mary Montgomery Koppel, Chris Florio,Thomas Palance.

A light reception of seasonal treats will be offered during intermission compliments of Russell Orchards. Due to the size of the ballroom, seating is very limited and early reservations are highly recommended.
