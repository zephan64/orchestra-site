The Orchestra on the Hill, led by Artistic Director Thomas Palance, presents **Images 26: From Chaos to Unity**—an immersive, multidisciplinary performance marking the orchestra’s first public concert at Ipswich’s new **Ipswich Community House**. Created in collaboration with **MustArt Studios**, this powerful production weaves together original music, visual art, poetry, narration, and dance by exceptional local artists.

Audiences will experience a moving journey from fragmentation to harmony, celebrating creativity, resilience, and human connection. Featured composers include Joe Mulholland (Berklee College of Music), G. Paul Naeger (New England Conservatory), and Thomas Palance. Movement is choreographed by Portland, ME-based dancer Anna Lisa Ledson, with narration and vocal performance by acclaimed vocalist and composer Brittany Betts. The evening concludes with a unifying composition by Chris Florio.

Join us for an uplifting and unforgettable artistic experience.
